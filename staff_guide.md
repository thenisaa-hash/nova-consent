## 2. STRATEGIC LEGAL ANALYSIS: JOE'S CLAIMS VS. NEW DEFENSES

Joe cited three specific legal frameworks. Here is what they mean and how our new form stops them:

### **A. PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act 2012)**
*   **Joe's Claim:** His personal data (video/image/opinion) was shared with "Furniture City" without consent because he only consented to "X'clusive Home".
*   **The Law:** Under PDPA, transferring data between legal entities (even sister companies or subsidiaries) without explicit consent is a breach of the "Purpose Limitation" and "Consent" obligations.
*   **Our New Defense:** **Section 1 and 2** of the new agreement explicitly mention the parent company (**Nova Furnishing Holdings Pte Ltd**) and list all sister brands/entities (*Nova Furnishing Centre Pte Ltd (NOVA)*, *Rozel Furnishing Pte Ltd (ROZEL)*, *X'clusive Home Pte Ltd (X'clusive Home)*, *Mega Home Furnishing Pte Ltd (Mega Home)*, and *Sofaland Pte Ltd (The Sofa Colony)*). It gets the customer to sign off on cross-brand usage, making it legal to share and publish the content across all group channels.

### **B. CPFTA (Consumer Protection (Fair Trading) Act 2012)**
*   **Joe's Claim:** Posting his video on Furniture City's page is "false advertising" because he bought from X'clusive Home. He argues that Furniture City is misleading consumers by representing his endorsement as their own.
*   **The Law:** CPFTA protects consumers against unfair or misleading practices. If a customer endorses Brand A, displaying it as an endorsement for Brand B might be deemed misleading.
*   **Our New Defense:** **Section 3** of the form explicitly states that the Group operates as a unified business entity and that publishing the content on sister brand pages is acceptable, does not constitute a misleading representation under CPFTA, and that the customer waives any right to claim otherwise.

### **C. SCAP (Singapore Code of Advertising Practice) / ASAS**
*   **Joe's Claim:** ASAS handles complaints regarding misleading ads under SCAP. Testimonials must be genuine and used with the consent of the person shown.
*   **Our New Defense:** **Section 3 & 4** require the customer to confirm that their statements are genuine and honest, and they grant perpetual, worldwide rights to the Group to display it. Since ASAS requires proof of permission when challenged, having a signed copy of this agreement is an absolute defense.

## 3. SOP FOR FUTURE CONTENT SHOOTS

To ensure this never happens again, implement this 4-step workflow:

### **Step 1: The "Digital-First" Consent Capture**
*   **Action:** When a videographer/staff goes to shoot a testimonial, they **must not** record until this agreement is signed.
*   **Implementation:** Keep the HTML version of the consent form (`consent_form_online.html`) bookmarked on a store iPad/tablet. 
*   **Process:** 
    1. Open the form on the iPad.
    2. Fill in the Customer's Name, contact number, and email.
    3. Let them sign directly on the screen (using a stylus or finger).
    4. Submitting will save the data locally.

### **Step 2: File Naming & Organization**
*   **Action:** Store the media file and the consent form together.
*   **Naming Convention:**
    `[YYYYMMDD]_[Brand]_[CustomerName]_Consent` (e.g. Exported CSV logs will serve as the database).
*   **Storage:** Keep the exported database logs secure on the company Google Drive/OneDrive folder named `Marketing_Consent_Records`.

### **Step 3: Marketing Approval Gates**
*   **Action:** Before any video editor or social media manager uploads a customer testimonial to *any* page (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, etc.), they must verify that a corresponding consent record exists in the `Marketing_Consent_Records` log.
*   **No Log Record = No Post.** No exceptions.
